IC-FCSEE 2027

IC-FCSEE 2027 is an interdisciplinary international conference organized by Sphoorthy Engineering College, Nadergul, Hyderabad, Telangana, India scheduled for 19–20 March 2027 in Hybrid Mode.

With the theme “Converging Intelligence, Security and Sustainability for a Transformative Future”, IC-FCSEE 2027 brings together researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, research scholars, educators, and students to exchange knowledge, present innovative research, and explore emerging technologies, and address contemporary global challenges.

The conference brings together diverse and rapidly evolving research areas spanning Advanced Computing and Artificial Intelligence, Educational Transformations and EduTech Innovations, Cybersecurity and Autonomous Systems, and Deep-Tech, Quantum and Sustainable Technologies. By connecting these domains, IC-FCSEE 2027 encourages interdisciplinary research that bridges technological innovation, educational advancement, digital security, intelligent systems, and sustainable development.

Recognizing that many of today's complex challenges cannot be addressed through a single discipline, the conference promotes interdisciplinary collaboration, knowledge exchange, academic–industry engagement, and innovation. It provides a platform for participants to present original research, discuss emerging trends, develop professional and research collaborations, and explore opportunities to translate innovative ideas into meaningful real-world applications.

About the Sphoorthy Engineering College (SPHN)

Established in 2004, Sphoorthy Engineering College has been excelling in research and entrepreneurship ever since its inception. In the last decade, it has given hundreds of engineers and leaders to the nation.

The college is well known for its innovative spirit, intensive research and hands-on approach to solving real-world problems, producing skilful engineers who are well received by industry and academia. The institution is approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and permanently affiliated to Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU), Hyderabad, Telangana, India. It is accredited with an "A Grade" by NAAC, and provides state-of-the-art facilities and infrastructure for promoting technological advancement and innovation.

Sphoorthy Engineering College continues to strive to be a learning hub for students, equipping them with on-demand skills to be readily employable across diversified career pathways — encouraging collaborative learning between industry and academia to reinforce its curriculum with practical, real-world experience.

Explore Hyderabad – The City of Pearls

Hyderabad, the capital of Telangana, is a vibrant city where centuries of history, rich culture, traditional cuisine, and modern technology come together. The city is renowned for iconic landmarks such as Charminar, Golconda Fort, Chowmahalla Palace, and Falaknuma Palace.

Hyderabad provides an excellent setting for international conferences, academic events, research collaborations, and professional gatherings. Its combination of heritage, modern infrastructure, connectivity, hospitality, and cultural experiences makes it an attractive destination for conference participants.

Major Attractions in Hyderabad

Participants attending IC-FSCEE 2027 can experience the cultural richness, historical heritage, and modern attractions of Hyderabad.

Charminar

An iconic symbol of Hyderabad and a remarkable example of Indo-Islamic architecture.

Golconda Fort

A magnificent historic fortress known for its architecture, history, and remarkable acoustic features.

Hussain Sagar Lake

A major scenic attraction known for its lakefront surroundings, Buddha statue, boating, and recreational destinations.

Chowmahalla Palace

A historic palace complex representing Hyderabad's royal heritage and architectural traditions.

Falaknuma Palace

A magnificent historic palace known for its grand architecture, royal heritage, and timeless elegance.

One of Hyderabad's major cultural attractions featuring an extensive collection of art, antiques, and historical objects.
